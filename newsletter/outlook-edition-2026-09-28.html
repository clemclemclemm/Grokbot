What changed

Three signals (access 2026-09-28):

Brief 1 · Business credit (ABS). Other private non-financial corporations demanded $73.5b of credit in Jun qtr 2026: loans $43.8b, equity $23.6b, bonds $7.2b (Source fact, ABS Finance and Wealth). Derived: loans ≈ 59.6% (43.8 / 73.5). NFC investment +$9.2b to $90.8b; capital investment 25.1% of GDP (Source fact). No manuf cut. Not an all-time record (Mar 2022 private NF was $160.7b).

Brief 2 · Cognex / RealSense (OEM). Cognex agreed (22 Sep 2026) to buy RealSense for about US$500m cash; expected close Q4 2026 (Source fact: OEM deal terms, Cognex PR). RealSense 2026 revenue outlook US$80-90m; Cognex cites robotic perception ~US$600m today to ~US$1.6b by 2030 (Source fact: OEM forward / market estimate).

Brief 3 · Cobots lag industrial (IFR). Industrial installs 603,000 (+11%); stock 5.079 million (+9%); new cobots 66,000 (= 11% of industrial installs) at +7%, below the industrial market for the first time in IFR's cobot series (Source fact, IFR President Report / WR 2026, stats year 2025). Derived: gap 4 ppt (11 − 7). Metal and machinery +22%; electronics and automotive each +10% (Source fact).

Sources: ABS Finance and Wealth · Cognex PR (22 Sep 2026) · IFR President Report (24 Sep 2026).

Why it matters

PME boss, plant chef, and fund share one CapEx question: is funding open, and which cell type is growing?

Private non-financial credit is loan-heavy while capital formation stays about a quarter of GDP. That is a kit funding window, not a household-mortgage story.

Cobots are no longer the fastest industrial story. Metal and machinery lead at +22%. Collaborative-by-default bets against the IFR mix. Cognex buying depth sensing is CapEx pressure, not proof your plant needs it yet.

Short reportage

Private non-financial businesses took $73.5b of credit in the June quarter while capital investment held at 25.1% of GDP. Boards that only hear household mortgages miss the ABS print: lenders and equity still fund corporate CapEx.

IFR closes the pack. Cobots +7% while industrial installs +11%, with metal and machinery +22%, means collaborative is no longer the automatic growth bet. Size industrial or metal cells to dated bottlenecks while funding is open.

Cognex-RealSense is pressure, not the thesis. Useful only after your scrap series exists, and after the deal closes.
